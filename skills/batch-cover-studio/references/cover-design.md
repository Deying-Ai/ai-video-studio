# Cover Design Rules

## Default format

- Canvas: `1080x1440`
- Aspect ratio: 3:4
- Subject: actual speaker frame
- Primary title: two to four lines
- Contrast: readable at approximately 360 pixels wide

## Safe zones

Keep critical text inside:

- Left and right padding: at least 64 pixels
- Top padding: at least 120 pixels
- Bottom padding: at least 200 pixels

Platform controls and profile overlays vary. Keep faces and title text away from edges.

## Layout styles

### Bottom

Use when the speaker's face occupies the upper half. Place the title card in the lower third with a dark translucent panel.

### Top

Use when the face is centered or low. Place the title card near the top while keeping the eyes unobstructed.

### Center

Use only when the background has enough negative space. The center panel must not cover the mouth or eyes.

## Text hierarchy

1. Eyebrow: topic, series, or concrete category.
2. Title: one tension and one promise.
3. Brand: small and secondary.

Avoid:

- Multiple unrelated claims
- Fake urgency
- Tiny explanatory paragraphs
- Decorative fonts that reduce readability
- Repeating the full video title when a shorter cover line is stronger

## Batch strategy

Generate at least two variants for each approved title:

- Top versus bottom placement
- Short versus slightly more explanatory wording
- Different frame moments, when expression changes

Prefer six to twelve files total. More variants are useful only when they are genuinely different.

## Selection rubric

Score each variant:

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Face and eyes visible | 20 |
| Title readable at thumbnail size | 20 |
| Hook clarity | 20 |
| Visual hierarchy | 15 |
| Contrast | 15 |
| Platform safe-area compliance | 10 |

Recommend the three strongest, non-duplicate covers.
