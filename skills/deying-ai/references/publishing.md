# Publishing

## Title

- Keep the title under 20 Chinese characters when possible.
- Use the consequence or contradiction, not a generic topic label.
- Do not put the full script into the title.

## Caption

Keep the caption short and scan-friendly:

1. One sentence explaining the change or opportunity.
2. Three to five useful points.
3. One question inviting a specific comment.

Avoid promising guaranteed income or guaranteed platform results.

## Hashtags

Use five topical tags. Mix:

- one broad category
- one platform or market
- one policy or business term
- one AI or workflow term
- one audience or outcome term

Example:

`#跨境电商 #美国市场 #关税政策 #AI工具 #运营干货`

## Comment Prompt

Ask a question that can be answered from experience:

- `你现在做的是哪个平台？`
- `你最怕AI在哪一步出错？`
- `你会把这条流程先用在哪个环节？`

Avoid prompts that only ask for likes or use empty engagement bait.
