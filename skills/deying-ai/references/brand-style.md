# Brand Style

## Canvas

- Default size: `1080x1920`
- Default frame rate: `30fps`
- Default background: `#05080b`
- Grid: `#36a57c` at about `10%` opacity, approximately `34px`
- Main panel: `#0a1014` at about `96%` opacity
- Primary text: `#f4f0e8`
- Muted text: `#8c999f`
- Accent colors: green `#69f0b2`, orange `#ff7a45`, blue `#63a9ff`, yellow `#f3cc62`, red `#ff5e6c`

The background remains dark even when reference footage uses a bright interface. If a bright product UI is required, place it inside the central panel instead of replacing the page background.

## Layout

- Keep a top label bar with the series name and topic tag.
- Place the circular speaker near the upper-right corner.
- Keep the speaker around `158-190px` in diameter. Do not let it dominate the main idea.
- Start the main panel around `y=390` and keep its content in the central safe area.
- Use one main idea per scene. Use a short eyebrow label, a strong two-line title, and supporting cards.
- Keep all text inside its parent card with comfortable padding.

## Speaker Window

- Use the source video, not a still frame.
- Crop a square around the person's face before scaling to the circle.
- Keep the face at the center of the crop.
- Add only restrained motion: a small float, pulse, or ring treatment.
- The speaker must never cover the central text panel.

## Motion

Use two to four motion ideas per scene, not every available effect.

- Scene entrance: quick fade, spring scale, and a small vertical translate.
- Camera: slow push, crop shift, or panel reveal.
- Data: number count, line sweep, or card sequence.
- Explanation: connector line draw, highlighted row, or active step.
- Transition: match cut, slide, mask reveal, or brief flare.

Animation should follow the narration:

- Put the hook on the screen during the first `1-3s`.
- Change the dominant card when the speaker introduces a new claim.
- Highlight one rule or step at a time.
- Stop motion before the viewer needs to read a dense sentence.

## Typography

- Prefer `STHeiti`, `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, or a local Chinese sans-serif fallback.
- Use large, heavy titles and smaller muted detail text.
- Use English labels only as small supporting metadata, never as the sole carrier of meaning.
- Keep letter spacing at zero for normal body copy.

## Avoid

- Static avatar screenshots
- Face cut off by the circle
- Bottom subtitles in the default template
- Card-inside-card stacking
- Dense text that requires pausing the video to read
- Excessive particle effects, orbs, or unrelated decoration
- Copying another creator's exact frame composition
