# Delivery Checklist

- Final MP4 exists and opens correctly.
- Video is 9:16 unless the brief says otherwise.
- Audio is present, synchronized, and free of clipping.
- Speaker remains face-centered and unobstructed.
- All factual claims are supported.
- Music, footage, fonts, logos, voices, and likenesses are authorized.
- Cover files are readable at thumbnail size.
- Cover text stays inside safe areas.
- `qa-report.md` contains a final approval.
- `delivery-notes.md` lists every deliverable path.
- Publishing copy exists only when requested.
- Nothing was published automatically.
- No unavailable TTS, digital-human, voice-clone, or lip-sync capability is implied.
