# Review Rubric

Review the actual media, not only the production plan or encode log.

## Critical gates

All must pass:

- The speaker is visible, centered, and unobstructed.
- The audio is intelligible with no clipping or missing sections.
- Text stays inside safe areas and does not cover the face.
- Factual claims are supported.
- The output duration and aspect ratio match the brief.
- No unauthorized voice, likeness, footage, music, or logo is used.

Any failed critical gate means **do not publish**.

## Score

| Category | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Hook and first three seconds | 20 |
| One clear thesis and logical flow | 20 |
| Save or practical value | 15 |
| Visual hierarchy and readability | 15 |
| Speaker treatment and motion | 10 |
| Audio and pacing | 10 |
| CTA and platform fit | 10 |

Approval threshold: 85/100 with every critical gate passed.
